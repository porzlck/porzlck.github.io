ABC 483 G.Qualification

发表于2026-11-07|更新于2026-11-07|算法

You are given integers N,KN,KN,K. It is guaranteed that the given integers satisfy K≤N2\displaystyle K \le \frac N2K≤2N​.

For permutations P=(P1,P2,…,PN),Q=(Q1,Q2,…,QN)P=(P_1,P_2,\ldots,P_N),Q=(Q_1,Q_2,\ldots,Q_N)P=(P1​,P2​,…,PN​),Q=(Q1​,Q2​,…,QN​) of (1,2,…,N)(1,2,\ldots,N)(1,2,…,N), define f(P,Q)f(P,Q)f(P,Q) as follows:

Consider choosing 2K2K2K of these NNN people by the following 222 selection methods.

  • Method ①: Choose the KKK people with the best ranks in contest A. Then, among the N−KN-KN−K people not yet chosen, choose the KKK people with the best ranks in contest B.
  • Method ②: Choose the KKK people with the best ranks in contest B. Then, among the N−KN-KN−K people not yet chosen, choose the KKK people with the best ranks in contest A.

Let f(P,Q)f(P,Q)f(P,Q) be the number of people who are chosen by method ① but not chosen by method ②.

There are N!2N!^2N!2 pairs (P,Q)(P,Q)(P,Q) of permutations of (1,2,…,N)(1,2,\ldots,N)(1,2,…,N). Find the sum of f(P,Q)f(P,Q)f(P,Q) over all of them, modulo 998244353998244353998244353.

You are given TTT test cases; solve each of them.

  • 1≤T≤2×1051\le T\le 2\times 10^51≤T≤2×105
  • 2≤N≤1062\le N\le 10^62≤N≤106
  • 1≤K≤N2\displaystyle 1\le K \le \frac N21≤K≤2N​
  • The sum of NNN over all test cases is at most 10610^6106.

思路

枚举两场比赛前 KKK 名的交集大小 iii。

两种方法都会选中前 KKK 名的并集，共 2K−i2K-i2K−i 人，因此还需要从剩余的 N−2K+iN-2K+iN−2K+i 人中各选 iii 人。

令 p=N−2Kp=N-2Kp=N−2K，对于方法二未选中的 ppp 人，每个人被方法一选中的概率为 ip+i\frac{i}{p+i}p+ii​，因此平均贡献为：

pip+i\frac{pi}{p+i} p+ipi​

接下来计算交集大小恰好为 iii 的排列对数。

先确定比赛 A 的排列，有 N!N!N! 种。比赛 B 的前 KKK 名需要从 A 的前 KKK 名中选 iii 人，再从剩余 N−KN-KN−K 人中选 K−iK-iK−i 人，最后考虑 B 的具体排名，因此方案数为：

N!(Ki)(N−KK−i)K!(N−K)!N!\binom Ki\binom{N-K}{K-i}K!(N-K)! N!(iK​)(K−iN−K​)K!(N−K)!

乘上平均贡献并枚举 iii，得到：

ans=N!K!(N−K)!∑i=1K(Ki)(N−KK−i)pip+ians=N!K!(N-K)!\sum_{i=1}^{K}\binom Ki\binom{N-K}{K-i}\frac{pi}{p+i} ans=N!K!(N−K)!i=1∑K​(iK​)(K−iN−K​)p+ipi​

当 N=2KN=2KN=2K 时答案为 000。预处理阶乘和逆阶乘即可快速计算组合数及模逆元。
